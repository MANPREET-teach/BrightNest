About BrightNest

Cleaner homes. Healthier habits.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first BrightNest visit. Still curious? Send us a message using the form below.

What's included in a standard clean?

A standard clean covers dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom sanitizing, and a general tidy-up of all main living areas. You'll receive our full checklist in your booking confirmation.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No — we bring all our own eco-friendly supplies and equipment. If you have a preferred product or a sensitivity to a specific ingredient, just let us know when you book.

Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?

Yes. Every BrightNest professional is background-checked and covered by our liability insurance. We also provide ongoing training on our checklist and safety standards.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Absolutely. You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. Changes made within 24 hours may be subject to a small fee.

Do you bring pets into consideration?

We love pets! Our products are pet-safe, and our team is trained to work calmly around animals. Just let us know about any pets in the home when you book.

What is your satisfaction guarantee?

If something wasn't cleaned to your satisfaction, let us know within 48 hours and we'll come back and re-clean the affected area free of charge. Your peace of mind matters to us.
